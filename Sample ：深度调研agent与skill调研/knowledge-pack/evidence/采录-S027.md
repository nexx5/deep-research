## 摘要

USTC 团队发布 Deep Research Bench II 评测基准（arXiv:2601.08536，v2 更新于 2026-01-30）：以 132 个 grounded 研究任务（22 领域）评估深度调研系统（DRS）生成的长文报告，采用 9430 个细粒度二元 rubric（覆盖信息召回/分析/呈现三维度），rubric 全部派生自专家撰写调查文章并经四阶段 LLM+human 管线（含 400+ 人小时专家审查）构建；在多个 SOTA deep-research 系统上的评估显示最强模型满足率不足 50%。

## evidence

### 证据1（DRS 目标定义）
- source: S027
- location: arXiv 摘要（raw-S027.md 摘要段）
- quote: "Deep Research Systems (DRS) aim to help users search the web, synthesize information, and deliver comprehensive investigative reports."
- context: 论文开篇对 DRS 的功能定位

### 证据2（两类失败模式）
- source: S027
- location: arXiv 摘要（raw-S027.md 摘要段）
- quote: "Existing deep-research benchmarks often fall into two failure modes. Some do not adequately test a system's ability to analyze evidence and write coherent reports. Others rely on evaluation criteria that are either overly coarse or directly defined by LLMs (or both), leading to scores that can be biased relative to human experts and are hard to verify or interpret."
- context: 论文指出现有基准两大问题，作为提出新基准的动机

### 证据3（任务集与评估方式）
- source: S027
- location: arXiv 摘要（raw-S027.md 摘要段）
- quote: "It contains 132 grounded research tasks across 22 domains; for each task, a system must produce a long-form research report that is evaluated by a set of 9430 fine-grained binary rubrics in total, covering three dimensions: information recall, analysis, and presentation."
- context: DRB II 的规模与评估机制核心描述

### 证据4（rubric 来源与构建管线）
- source: S027
- location: arXiv 摘要（raw-S027.md 摘要段）
- quote: "All rubrics are derived from carefully selected expert-written investigative articles and are constructed through a four-stage LLM+human pipeline that combines automatic extraction with over 400 human-hours of expert review, ensuring that the criteria are atomic, verifiable, and aligned with human expert judgment."
- context: rubric 构建方法学（专家文章派生 + 四阶段 LLM+human 管线 + 专家审查）

### 证据5（评测结果）
- source: S027
- location: arXiv 摘要（raw-S027.md 摘要段）
- quote: "We evaluate several state-of-the-art deep-research systems on Deep Research Bench II and find that even the strongest models satisfy fewer than 50% of the rubrics, revealing a substantial gap between current DRSs and human experts."
- context: 评测结论——SOTA 系统与人类专家存在实质差距
