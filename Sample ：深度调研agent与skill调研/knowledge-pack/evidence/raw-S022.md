## 3. Genspark 官方帮助中心（https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans）

> 抓取成功 2026-08-04T17:11。官方帮助中心公开会员计划结构，但**美元价格不公开**（登录后的 Plan 页 `/me?open_pricing=help_center` 才显示）。

### Plan Overview（原文）
"Genspark offers three membership tiers to suit different needs:
- **Free Plan** — Basic access with standard features
- **Plus Plan** — Enhanced features and higher credit allocation
- **Pro Plan** — Professional-level access and resources"

### Plus Benefits（原文摘录）
- **Credits**: "Plus offers multiple credit tiers, **starting from 10,000 credits per month**. Higher tiers provide more credits at higher price points — you can choose your tier when subscribing or switch anytime."
- **All SOTA Models**: "access to all the latest state-of-the-art text, image, video, and audio generation models"
- **Professional Workspace**: "Full access to AI Slides, Genspark Code, and more agents; 50 GB AI Drive storage; Commercial use of all AI-generated content"
- **Unlimited Usage**: "Unlimited core chat in AI Chat Agent, zero credit cost; Unlimited image creation in AI Image Agent, zero credit cost; Unlimited voice dictation in Speakly, zero credit cost"
- **"*All benefits are guaranteed until December 31, 2026*"**

### Pro Benefits（原文摘录）
- **Credits**: "Pro offers multiple credit tiers, **starting from 125,000 credits per month**."
- **Professional Workspace**: "1 TB AI Drive storage *(Pro Exclusive)*"
- **Unlimited Usage**: 同 Plus；"exclusive access to Nano Banana Pro (4K), Nano Banana 2 (4K), and GPT Image 2 (High quality)"
- **"*All benefits are guaranteed until December 31, 2026*"**

### 计费口径（原文摘录）
- 订阅入口：Plan 页（/me?open_pricing=help_center），月度/年度（"Annual plan — billed as a lump sum for the full year upfront (**saves ~20%** compared to monthly); credits are issued on a monthly basis throughout the year"）
- 升级（Plus→Pro）：立即生效，按剩余周期比例收费；差额 credits 立即到账，本周期内有效
- 降级（Pro→Plus）：当前计划持续到账单周期结束，新计划下周期生效；**credits 不结转**
- 取消：当前计划保持到周期结束，之后自动回退 Free plan（100 daily credits）
- 支付：Stripe；销售额外税费/汇率/跨境费由银行/税局收取

### Genspark Cloud Computer（Claw 部署，官方原文）
"Genspark Claw runs on Genspark Cloud Computer for deployment. To use Genspark Claw, you'll need an active Genspark Cloud Computer subscription."
| Tier | Specs |
| --- | --- |
| Lite | 2 vCPU / 4GB RAM / 64GB Storage |
| Standard | 2 vCPU / 8GB RAM / 64GB Storage |
| Powerful | 4 vCPU / 16GB RAM / 128GB Storage |
"some Claw actions may use Genspark credits. Credits are shared and managed in one place across Genspark."

### 退款政策（原文摘要）
- EU/UK/Turkey：购买后 14 天内可退（月付与年付均适用）
- 其他客户：月付 24 小时内、年付 72 小时内
- 7 个工作日处理；检测到过量使用可能拒绝
