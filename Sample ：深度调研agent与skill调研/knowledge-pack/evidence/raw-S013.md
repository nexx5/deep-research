# 源A：DeepResearch Bench（学术独立基准）

## A0. 元信息
- URL: https://deepresearch-bench.github.io
- 论文: arXiv:2506.11763（Du, Mingxuan; Xu, Benfeng; Zhu, Chiwei; Wang, Xiaorui; Mao, Zhendong）
- 机构: 1University of Science and Technology of China, 2Metastone Technology, Beijing, China
- 开源: 代码 GitHub（Ayanami0730/deep_research_bench）、Leaderboard（Hugging Face: muset-ai/DeepResearch-Bench-Leaderboard）、Data 开放
- 授权: 网站 CC BY-SA 4.0
- 评测版本窗口: 论文为 2025 年发表；评测对象为"早期发布的 Deep Research Agents"（详见 A3）

## A1. 基准构建（方法与样本）
- "a benchmark consisting of 100 PhD-level research tasks, each meticulously crafted by domain experts across 22 distinct fields"——100 个 PhD 级研究任务，由领域专家（博士或 5 年以上资深从业者）构建，覆盖 22 个领域；50 中文 + 50 英文。
- 任务来源分析："analyzed 96,147 raw user queries from interactions with web search-enabled LLM Chatbot. After filtering... identified 44,019 relevant queries. We then employed DeepSeek-V3-0324 to classify these queries into 22 distinct topic domains."
- 分布: "Science & Technology and Business & Finance represent the highest proportions of deep research queries."

## A2. 两套评测框架
- **RACE**（Reference-based Adaptive Criteria-driven Evaluation framework with Dynamic Weighting）：评估生成研究报告质量，四个维度 Comp.（Comprehensiveness）/ Depth / Inst.（Instruction-following）/ Read.（Readability），总分 Overall。
- **FACT**（Framework for Factual Abundance and Citation Trustworthiness）：评估信息检索有效性，两个指标 C. Acc.（Citation Accuracy 引用准确率）/ E. Cit.（Effective Citations 有效引用数）。
- 人类一致性验证: "recruited 70+ annotators with Master's degrees and relevant domain expertise... For each of the 50 Chinese tasks, three domain-expert annotators independently scored reports generated by four distinct agents."（70+ 硕士级标注者；50 中文任务 × 4 个 agent × 3 人独立评分）

## A3. 主要结果（表 1，Deep Research Agent 组）
| 模型 | RACE Overall | Comp. | Depth | Inst. | Read. | C. Acc. | E. Cit. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grok Deeper Search | 40.24 | 37.97 | 35.37 | 46.30 | 44.05 | 83.59 | 8.15 |
| Perplexity Deep Research | 42.25 | 40.69 | 39.39 | 46.40 | 44.28 | **90.24** | 31.26 |
| Gemini-2.5-Pro Deep Research | **48.88** | **48.53** | **48.50** | 49.18 | **49.44** | 81.44 | **111.21** |
| OpenAI Deep Research | 46.98 | 46.87 | 45.25 | **49.27** | 47.14 | 77.96 | 40.79 |

- "RACE Framework Results: Gemini-2.5-Pro Deep Research demonstrated leading overall performance (48.88)... OpenAI Deep Research achieved the highest score in Instruction-Following (49.27)..."
- "FACT Framework Results: Deep Research Agents significantly outperformed LLMs with Search Tools in terms of Effective Citations. Gemini-2.5-Pro Deep Research achieved an exceptional 111.21 average effective citations... Perplexity Deep Research showed the highest Citation Accuracy (90.24%)..."
- LLM with Search Tools 对照组（节选）: Claude-3.7-Sonnet w/Search RACE 40.67 / C.Acc 93.68 / E.Cit 32.48；Claude-3.5-Sonnet w/Search C.Acc **94.04**；GPT-4o-Search-Preview(high) C.Acc 88.41。

## A4. 评测方法有效性
- 人类一致性对比（表 2）: Vanilla Prompt Overall 60.46% vs RACE(Full) **72.56%**（PAR 71.33 / OPC 99.54 / FAP 60.24 / FAS 59.12）；Ablation 显示去掉 reference-based 比较是最大降幅（68.19%）。
- Judge LLM 对比（表 3）: Gemini 2.5 Pro Preview 72.56% / $0.13 per query（被选中）；o3 68.58% / $0.37；o4-mini 71.63% / $0.04；Claude 3.7 Sonnet 72.28% / $0.47。

## A5. 利益相关
- 学术团队（USTC + Metastone），网站与论文未声明厂商赞助；无送测迹象。
- 与 AGI-Eval 平台合作（GitHub README 提及，2025-07-18）；联系方式 dumingxuan@mail.ustc.edu.cn / imlrz@mail.ustc.edu.cn（提交 leaderboard 排名需联系）。

# 源B：Section AI 实测（真实工作任务对比）

## B0. 元信息
- URL: https://www.sectionai.com/blog/chatgpt-vs-gemini-deep-research
- 作者: Ana Silva（Section AI，COO Chief of Staff）；日期 2025-03-14
- 背景: 为季度董事会会议准备材料，用 Deep Research 做自家新产品（AI 教练 ProfAI）的竞品分析

## B1. 评测方法（样本）
- 同一 prompt 双工具对比；prompt 包含：persona（Section 董事会成员视角）、希望覆盖的具体信息（产品描述/定价/市场牵引力等）、输出格式（便于董事会成员快速消化）。
- 任务类型: 竞品分析（1 个真实工作任务，n=1）。
- 主观评分维度: Tool usability（工具可用性）、Output quality（输出质量）。

## B2. 结果
- **OpenAI Deep Research（ChatGPT，o3）**: Tool usability 4/5；Output quality 4.5/5。
  - "ChatGPT's research went beyond the big edtech players and included more novel companies like Skillsoft and Datacamp – more accurate competitive solutions..."
  - "✅ It gave me exactly what I asked for – competitors that I may not have otherwise known about..."
  - "❌ ChatGPT's feature noted a lower volume of sources checked in its report than Gemini."
  - "❌ Every time I wanted to prompt Deep Research, I had to re-select the 'Deep Research' mode."
- **Gemini Deep Research（Gemini 1.5 Pro）**: Tool usability 5/5；Output quality 2.5/5。
  - "Gemini's output focused on edtech at a very high level, citing companies like Udemy, Coursera, and LinkedIn Learning – which didn't help me in my search for competitors I wasn't aware of."
  - "❌ The content quality of Gemini's output was much weaker... too high level to be immediately usable. It provided the kind of information I could pull from a quick Google search."
  - "❌ It also disregarded parts of the prompt I specifically asked for, like significant partnerships, market differentiation, and value propositions."
  - "✅ Gemini noted that it looked at a higher volume of sources in its report than ChatGPT cited."
  - "✅ Gemini created a more collaborative experience, presenting a research plan before it dove in..."
  - "✅ Perhaps the most valuable part of researching with Gemini: Its output can quickly be exported to a Google Doc..."
- **结论**: "Even though the user experience in Gemini was better and more organized, the win goes to ChatGPT... Ultimately, even though Gemini claims to reference more sources, it seems like ChatGPT's multimodal analysis yields better results. Ironic for an LLM run by a search engine!"
- 背景事实: "Google launched Gemini 1.5 Pro, featuring the Deep Research capability, in December 2024. OpenAI launched their Deep Research feature in February 2025, as an AI agent integrated into ChatGPT's o3 model."；"ChatGPT's Deep Research feature does multimodal analysis (including text, images, and PDFs) while Gemini's Deep Research feature only does text-based research."

## B3. 利益相关
- Section AI 为 AI 培训/咨询公司（销售 AI 转型服务），非厂商；作者为公司内部员工，为内部会议做研究，无厂商送测迹象。
- 样本仅 1 个任务、主观评分；作者提示使用建议（明确输出格式、认真回答澄清问题、明确目标）。

# 源C：AIMultiple 评测基准（DR-50 / DR-2T / Agent vs Deep Research Models）

## C0. 元信息
- URL: https://aimultiple.com/ai-deep-research
- 作者: Cem Dilmegani（AIMultiple Principal Analyst，自 2017 年）；页面更新 2026-06-22
- 性质: B2B AI 评测媒体/咨询机构（"AIMultiple informs hundreds of thousands of businesses... including 60% of Fortune 500 every month"），自建三个基准。

## C1. 基准一：Agent vs Deep Research Models（5 任务 / 33 检查点）
- 方法: 5 个跨领域研究任务；"Every checkpoint is scored binary: correct or wrong."；"Every question targets information published after the models training data cutoff dates. The benchmark was run in the first week of April 2026."；ground truth 来自主源（Unity 官方文档、Atlassian SEC 8-K、Paramount 新闻稿、ARC-AGI-3 arXiv 论文、Unity 升级指南）；"Scoring: automated pattern matching for numbers, dates, and names. LLM judge (GPT-4o) for explanation-quality checkpoints. Human reviewer validated all results."
- 工具接入: "Deep research models were called via OpenRouter API (o3, o4-mini, Sonar) and Parallel API. Agents were run via their CLI interfaces with web search enabled, no MCP tools. In Claude Code, we used Opus 4.6, and in Codex, we used GPT 5.4. Both in medium effort..."
- 结果:
  - "Parallel Ultra and Claude Code tied at the top with 97% accuracy. Codex followed at 93.9%. Perplexity Sonar scored 87.9%. The OpenAI deep research models (o3 and o4-mini) scored between 75.8% and 81.8%, despite running 27-125 web searches per task and costing 2-6x more than Sonar."
  - 成本: "Claude Code and Codex occupy the top-right corner: high accuracy at low cost ($1.54 and $1.30, respectively). Parallel achieves the same accuracy for $2.10. o3 costs $10.92 for 75.8% accuracy."
  - 延迟: "Claude Code is the fastest at 1.7 minutes per task on average. Parallel is slowest at 16.7 minutes but matches the top accuracy. Sonar sits in a strong middle position at 2.3 minutes and 87.9%."
  - 输出长度观察: "Sonar produces 5,253 words per task on average. Agents produce 398-483. Sonar wrote 4,509 words about the Unity EntityId struct, but could name one of its five public methods. Codex wrote 248 words and named all five... More words and more citations did not predict higher accuracy."
- Unity 6.3 迁移任务（Task 5）深挖:
  - "Three out of six tools returned system requirements for Unity 6.0 instead of Unity 6.3."（o3、o4-mini、Claude Code 均引用了 6.0 文档页）
  - "A team following o3's guide would target Android API 23 (Android 6.0). Unity 6.3 requires API 25 (Android 7.1). The build would fail... The guide itself looks professional: clean tables, sensible structure, correct tone. The numbers are wrong."
  - "Codex and Parallel both got every number right... It even identified that iOS minimum shifted from 12 to 13 within the 2022.3 line at build 2022.3.72f1..."
  - 结构性局限: "No tool read all four upgrade guides (6.0, 6.1, 6.2, 6.3) in sequence... Every tool found the most prominent page and extracted from that. This is a structural limitation for any research task that requires working through a series of related documents rather than finding a single answer."
  - Claude Code 方法: "spawned 4 parallel sub-agents... Fast (3 minutes 59 seconds), but the system requirements sub-agent fetched the wrong docs page."；Codex 方法: "ran 90 sequential web searches over 6 minutes 17 seconds... Every number was correct."；o3 方法: "spent 8 minutes and 32 web searches. It produced 2,132 words of general migration advice but pulled support timelines and system requirements from the 6.0 documentation. It did not mention any 6.3-specific breaking changes..."

## C2. 基准二：DR-50（Deep Research 50）Bench
- 方法: "50 questions across six question types"：Simple Factual Lookup / Comparative Analysis / Multi-Hop Reasoning / Calculation-Based / Structured JSON Extraction / Categorical Listing；"We compared each response against predefined ground-truth answers using GPT-4o-mini as an automated judge via OpenRouter."
- 结果: "Perplexity Sonar Deep Research shows the highest accuracy at 34% with moderate latency. Parallel Ultra and o4 mini deep research demonstrate similar accuracy levels around 22-24%, though Parallel Ultra requires significantly more time. o3-deep-research exhibits the lowest accuracy with extended latency."
- 引用数观察: "o4 mini deep research provides significantly more citations while maintaining efficiency... The minimal citations in o3 deep research, despite its premium cost, indicate that citation count isn't tied to resource consumption."

## C3. 基准三：DR-2T（Deep Research 2 Task）Bench
- Task 1（企业密码管理软件 5 项标准对比，要求表格输出）: "Nearly all tools provided detailed tables... **Gemini and Claude** emerged as the leading solutions, delivering extensive analytical reports... **Bright Data Deep Lookup** focused primarily on data extraction... **Kimi** employs a distinctive methodology... interactive report... **Note:** Perplexity provided a detailed report but failed to create a table with its gathered information. Since our prompt specifically requested table outputs, it received zero points for that task."
- Task 2（RPA 采用情况报告，测速度与来源覆盖）: "**Grok Deep Search** is approximately 10 times faster than **ChatGPT Deep Research** and searches approximately 3 times more webpages."；"**Claude Deep Search** is also highly responsive, having researched 261 sources in over 6 minutes."；"**Gemini** may not be an ideal choice for those seeking a fast and responsive solution, as it researched 62 sources in over 15 minutes."；"For example, Grok indexed more than 100 different pages in our second task... it took ~2 minutes for Grok."

## C4. 生态动态（页面其他陈述）
- Kimi K2.5 Deep Research: "supports end-to-end research and report generation for complex questions... The deep research is designed primarily for investment analysis, industry research, academic work, and strategic planning"（Agent Swarm 架构将单模型分解为多个专责子 agent 并行）。
- Gemini Deep Research 与 Google Workspace 集成（Gmail/Drive/Docs/Chat）、Chrome Auto Browse（Gemini 3）。
- "Microsoft has launched the public preview of Deep Research within Azure AI Foundry Agent Service, offering OpenAI's agentic research technology through Azure's enterprise platform."（用 o3-deep-research 模型，Bing Search grounding，API/SDK 可编程访问）。
- 引用 Gary Marcus 警告（"Deep Research, Deep Bullshit, and the potential (model) collapse of science"，AIMultiple 引用为对深度研究降低科学论文质量的担忧）。

## C5. 利益相关
- AIMultiple 是商业 B2B AI 研究/咨询机构（页面销售"automate a process with AI agents"企业服务），存在自营服务利益；方法论公开（50 题 6 类、GPT-4o-mini judge、人工验证、成本/延迟/引用统计口径均有描述）。
- 评测窗口: Agent vs DR 基准 2026 年 4 月第一周；页面更新 2026-06-22。
